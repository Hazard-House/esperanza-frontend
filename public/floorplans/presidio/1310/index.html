      Before you even step inside, you can make the Presidio feel like home with the choice of four statement elevation options. The single-story floorplan is spread over 1,148 sq. ft. and features three bedrooms and two bathrooms with the master boasting a walk-in closet and master bath. The hub of everyday life will surely be the open, light and spacious kitchen, dining and living zone with the option of adding a covered patio where you can host guests and overlook the backyard. For the eager home cook, there’s a center island, quality appliances and plenty of cabinetry in the kitchen while views over the dining area are ideal for entertaining friends. Built-in closets feature in the two secondary bedrooms plus there’s an attached one-car garage with laundry amenities for convenience. Depending on your needs and lifestyle, you might even choose to expand the garage to offer secure parking for two vehicles.
