      It’s Official: New Community at Tres Lagos!

      June 20, 2025

      Say hello to our newest community, Anaqua! Over 250 new homes are set to come to the growing Tres Lagos master plan development. Phase 1 of Anaqua will feature the release of 129 lots. Homes at Anaqua start from the low $300s and feature up to nearly 4,000 sq. ft. To celebrate, the first 10 customers to sign a contract in the new community will receive $10,000 in Flex Cash* to help make their dream home a reality.

      ANAQUA AT TRES LAGOS


      Groundbreaking Ceremony

      The official groundbreaking ceremony of Anaqua at Tres Lagos was held on June 4, 2025. As golden shovels turned over the dirt for the very first time in the new community, guests were treated to refreshments, swag bags, and the opportunity for a photo with Rhodie the Rhino.

      Guests learned about the community and heard speeches from key city leaders, officials, and Nick Rhodes, CEO and President of Rhodes and Esperanza Homes.

      Elizabeth Suarez, CEO of the McAllen Economic Development Corporation and McAllen Chamber of Commerce, reflected on the opportunities Esperanza Homes brings to McAllen families.

      “[This] project brings together economic opportunity for all our region. There's no better evidence of that than all the investment the team at Tres Lagos are doing and the part that they are to ensure that families like mine have an opportunity at this American dream.”

      Other speakers highlighted the community’s quality-of-life benefits, increased public safety access, and the opportunities the development brings for families of all price points.

      An Opportunity for Families

      Anaqua features new homes with modern designs and peaceful surroundings. Residents will enjoy a walkable community, steps away from top-rated schools like IDEA Public Schools and Texas A&M Higher Education Center at McAllen, retail and restaurants at The Shoppes at Tres Lagos, access to the Tres Lagos Community Center featuring a resort style swimming pool and indoor fitness center, along with all the amenities Tres Lagos has to offer like parks, trails, sport courts, and more.

      Your Home, Your Way

      Anaqua’s Phase 1 has 129 homesites available for purchase and offers homebuyers the ability to personalize their dream homes. Buyers can pick from 9 floor plans, 1 and 2-story homes, and their choice of elevation from Tuscan, Contemporary, Farmhouse, and Traditional. Personal finishes can be added through the one-of-a-kind Esperanza Homes Design Studio located within the master planned community.

      Every home is equipped with smart home technology and the latest in energy efficient materials and appliances. All homes built by Esperanza Homes meet the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)’s rigorous standards for Energy Star certification, providing homeowners with energy savings, lower maintenance bills, and peace of mind.
