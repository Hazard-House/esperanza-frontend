Discover Aquero: New Homes Now Selling in North Laredo

June 24, 2026


If you're searching for a new home in Laredo, now is the perfect time to explore Aquero by Esperanza Homes. Located in North Laredo next to Juan Ramirez Middle School, this growing community offers a convenient location with easy access to nearby trails, schools, and major highways.

Aquero features Esperanza Homes' popular Villas Home Collection, giving homebuyers the opportunity to build a home that fits their lifestyle. Choose from four thoughtfully designed floor plans—the Marzano,  Allegrini, Benanti, and Grapa—each available in Contemporary, Tuscan, and Traditional elevations. With homes starting from the high $200s, Aquero offers exceptional value without compromising quality, style, or comfort.

View Available Floor Plans



Every Esperanza home is built with energy-efficient features designed to help homeowners enjoy lasting comfort and potential savings. Combined with flexible floor plan options and personalized design selections, Aquero provides the opportunity to create a home that's uniquely yours.

Interest in Aquero continues to grow, and available homesites are limited. If you've been considering a move to North Laredo, now is the time to secure your preferred homesite and begin building your dream home.



Contact Esperanza Homes today to schedule a tour and learn more about available homesites in Aquero.
