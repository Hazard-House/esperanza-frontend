      McAllen, Texas

      Welcome to the City of McAllen, Texas. Known for its warm climate, rich cultural heritage, and thriving economy, McAllen offers a high quality of life that continues to attract new residents and businesses. With a unique blend of big-city amenities and small-town charm, the city has it all. It is home to top-rated schools, diverse shopping and dining options, and a strong job market in industries like healthcare, education, manufacturing and retail.

      Why We Chose to Build Here

      Prime Location

      Easy access to expressways, top schools, shopping, and entertainment.

      Vibrant Culture

      A rich heritage, lively art scene, and family-friendly atmosphere make McAllen something special. From authentic Mexican cuisine to the McAllen Holiday Parade, there is nothing like McAllen.

      More Home for Your Money

      McAllen’s lower cost of living means homebuyers get greater value without sacrificing quality.

      We Love the Valley

      Our roots are in the Rio Gande Valley and the City of McAllen is at the heart of the RGV.

      A Celebration of Culture and Heritage

      What truly sets the City of McAllen apart is its unique cultural scene. With a rich mix of Mexican and American influences, the city offers a vibrant and colorful blend of art, music, food, and traditions that celebrate its heritage and diversity. From mariachi festivals and Tex-Mex cuisine to museums and historical landmarks, there is always something to explore and discover in McAllen.

      A Highly Rated City

      McAllen consistently earns national recognition for its affordability, economic strength, and business-friendly environment. Ranked the second-best place to live in Texas by U.S. News & World Report (2024-2025), McAllen also boasts the lowest cost of living in the U.S. and a strong ‘AA+’ credit rating from Fitch. With a growing remote-work economy, a flourishing arts and entertainment scene, and easy access to destinations like South Padre Island and San Antonio, McAllen is a great place to call home.

      Life in McAllen, Texas

      McAllen has a vibrant mix of dining, entertainment and relaxation. From beautiful and informative museums, bird watching, and family filled events happening across the city, McAllen has it all. Read on to discover a few of our favorites.
