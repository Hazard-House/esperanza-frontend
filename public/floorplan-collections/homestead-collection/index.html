      Homestead Collection

      About This Collection

      The Homestead Home Collection offers magnificent living, with larger homesites and an average total square footage of 3,820. Designed for those looking to build their legacy, these homes feature flexible layouts and an impressive array of structural upgrades. Select homes also include butler pantries and spacious 3-car garages, adding an extra touch of luxury and convenience. With timeless charm and modern sophistication, this collection is perfect for creating a home that grows with your family.
