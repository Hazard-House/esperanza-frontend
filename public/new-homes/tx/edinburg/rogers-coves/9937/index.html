      Rogers Coves brings homebuyers' dreams to life in an intimate community that focuses on quality and connectivity in a desirable location in Edinburg, Texas. Located just off N. Expressway 281, Rogers Coves is designed with families in mind. This starter home community is surrounded by excellent schools and with close proximity to the University of Texas Rio Grande Valley (UTRGV), shopping centers, medical care, parks, and more. Featuring several floor plans in a variety of elevations and built with our E-Difference commitment, these award-winning homes are sure to fit your lifestyle.
