      About the Community

      Las Brisas at Tres Lagos is our newest community at Tres Lagos in McAllen, Texas. Located at the corner of Monte Cristo Road and Rooth Road, Las Brisas is just northeast of the Tres Lagos main entrance on Ware Road. This community offers easy access to the Shoppes at Tres Lagos, parks and playgrounds, dog parks, sports courts, walking trails, schools and so much more! With 423 home lots to choose from at Las Brisas at Tres Lagos, homeowners will find the perfect location for their dream home in South Texas. Build your home and experience the Esperanza Homes E-Difference. Personalize your home by choosing your lot location, floorplan, preferred elevation and home finishes. Plus, our energy-efficient designs are built to save on energy costs and minimize environmental impact, all while providing a comfortable, stylish and sustainable home.
