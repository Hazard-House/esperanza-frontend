      Welcome to Tres Lagos! A Master-Planned Community in McAllen, Texas
      Tres Lagos isn’t just a community, it’s a vibrant, master-planned destination where you can live, work, learn, and play. Designed for sustainable living, this 2,571-acre development offers everything you need in one beautiful location: parks, lakes, bike and walking trails, dog parks, schools, shopping, dining, and healthcare facilities.

      Find a home that fits your life.
      Tres Lagos offers a wide range of single-family homes for sale. Build and personalize your dream home, or explore move-in ready options. For added space and privacy, choose from half-acre to one-acre waterfront estate lots, perfect for a custom home that reflects your vision.

      From Texas A&M University and IDEA Public Schools to outdoor concerts at the amphitheater, there's always something to discover. With scenic views, modern amenities, and a welcoming atmosphere, Tres Lagos is where community and convenience come together.
