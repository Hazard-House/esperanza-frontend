Villas Las Lagunas is a new gated community bringing modern, low-maintenance living to Brownsville. Ideally located near Alton Gloor Boulevard and Paredes Line Road, this thoughtfully designed community offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and elevated style. At Villas Las Lagunas, residents enjoy a relaxed, low-maintenance lifestyle surrounded by lush landscaping and resort-style amenities. From a clubhouse with fitness center and sparkling pool to pickleball and basketball courts, plus a community park, and scenic walking trails, there’s something for everyone to enjoy. Featuring our Villas Home Collection, these energy-efficient homes offer one- and two-story floor plans with modern finishes, smart home technology, and fiber connectivity designed to keep you comfortable and connected. Discover the laid-back lifestyle you’ve been waiting for at Villas Las Lagunas.
